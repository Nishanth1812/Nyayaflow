BuildWhatMovesIndia — Hackathon Submission Brief

How NyayaFlow addresses every hackathon rubric requirement

1. Who is facing the problem?

Vulnerable citizens entitled to direct welfare benefits: rural farmers (PM-KISAN ₹2,000 DBT), formal workers (EPFO PF withdrawals), individual taxpayers (ITR refunds), and first-generation students (NSP scholarships).

2. What is difficult about the current experience?

"Disposal ≠ Resolution": Portals close tickets without money reaching bank accounts. Prerequisite blockers (like inactive NPCI mapping or UAN name mismatches) are opaque to citizens.

3. What did we change?

Introduced deterministic ordered prerequisite diagnostics, explainable keyword department routing, and post-disposal citizen resolution verification with 1-click First Appeal drafting.

4. Why is NyayaFlow better?

Shifts the core metric from administrative "Paper Disposal Rate" to "True Citizen Resolution Rate", with 0% LLM hallucination risk (100% deterministic rules).

5. What works today vs what is synthetic/mocked?

Real & Fully Functional: Full diagnostic engine, explainable router, evidence meter, SQLite case lifecycle database, appeal generator, and public analytics API.
Synthetic Data: Privacy-safe mock citizen IDs & simulated department timeline transitions.

6. How does this scale safely to 1 Billion citizens?

Ultra-lightweight architecture (runs on low-end servers, offline-ready), voice input for rural users, multi-lingual Indic support, and direct API interoperability with central CPGRAMS.
